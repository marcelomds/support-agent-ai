# Cancellation policy

## When you can cancel
You can cancel your StreamBox subscription at any time. There is no cancellation fee and no minimum contract period, on monthly or yearly plans.

## What happens after you cancel
Your access continues until the end of the period you already paid for. After that date the subscription ends and you are not charged again. Canceling does not trigger a refund for the current period.

## Changing your mind
You can undo a cancellation at any time before the period ends by contacting support. After the period ends you need to subscribe again.

## Suspended subscriptions
If a subscription is suspended because of failed payments, canceling it ends it immediately, because there is no paid period left.
