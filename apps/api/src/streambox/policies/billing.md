# Billing and failed payments

## When you are charged
You are charged at the start of each billing period: every 30 days on monthly plans and every 365 days on yearly plans.

## Failed payments
If a charge fails, the subscription becomes past due and we retry the charge over the following days. You keep access while retries are pending.

## Suspension
After 3 failed charges the subscription is suspended and access is blocked. To restore access, update your payment method in Account settings; the next successful charge reactivates the subscription.

## Payment methods
StreamBox accepts credit and debit cards. Support agents cannot see or change card details; they are updated only in Account settings.
