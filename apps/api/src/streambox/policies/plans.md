# Plans and plan changes

## Available plans
StreamBox has three tiers. Basic includes 1 screen. Standard includes 2 screens and offline downloads. Premium includes 4 screens, offline downloads and exclusive content. Each tier can be billed monthly or yearly; yearly costs the same as 10 months.

## Upgrades
An upgrade takes effect immediately. When you stay on the same billing interval, you pay only the price difference for the rest of the current period. When you move from monthly to yearly, a new yearly period starts on the day of the change and the full yearly price is charged.

## Downgrades
A downgrade takes effect at the start of the next billing period. Until then you keep the features of your current plan. There is no refund for the difference.

## Screens
The number of screens is how many devices can play at the same time. You can sign in on as many devices as you like.
