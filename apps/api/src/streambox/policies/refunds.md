# Refund policy

## The 7-day window
You can get a full refund of a charge within 7 days of the date it was made. This applies to monthly and yearly plans, and to renewals. Partial refunds are not offered.

## After 7 days
Charges older than 7 days are not refundable. The only exception is a duplicate charge.

## Duplicate charges
If you were charged twice for the same period (two successful charges of the same amount within 24 hours), the extra charge is refunded regardless of how long ago it happened.

## Refunds that need review
Refunds above $50.00 are reviewed by a member of the support team before they are issued. Reviews usually take up to 2 business days. You are notified when a decision is made.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method. They usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on the bank.

## Failed charges
A failed charge takes no money, so there is nothing to refund.
